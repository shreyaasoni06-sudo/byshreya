Shreya is a trend forecaster, writer and consultant based in London. Drawing on a background in psychology, literature and journalism, she decodes human behaviour, helping organisations navigate shifts in culture. She's particularly drawn to stories that get left out of the mainstream narrative, and approaches her research with an anthropological eye, always asking what a behaviour reveals about what lies underneath.
